                                                                          B. Tuning and Design Trade-offs
                                                                             Engineering judgement was heavily exercised during the
                                                                          tuning of the Q and R weighting matrices. The Q matrix
                                                                          was tuned to heavily penalize positional tracking errors,
                                                                          particularly depth and pitch (Qz = 100, Qθ = 500), en-
                                                                          suring tight regulation and rapid disturbance rejection. The
                                                                          integral states were assigned moderate weights to provide
                                                                          sufficient corrective action without inducing low-frequency
                                                                          oscillations.
                                                                             The control effort penalty R = 10I4×4 was chosen to
                                                                          deliberately allow aggressive transient responses. Lowering
                                                                          R further resulted in unacceptable high-frequency thruster
                                                                          chatter, representing a critical trade-off between tracking
                                                                          speed and actuator longevity.

                         VI. C ONCLUSION
   This report successfully engineered a comprehensive con-
trol architecture for an underactuated ROV, explicitly balanc-
ing theoretical design against practical subsea limitations.
A 3-DOF planar model was derived and validated. A 20
Hz LQI controller augmented with anti-windup provided
exceptional, robust waypoint tracking, effortlessly handling
actuator saturation and severe mismatched blackbox dynam-
ics. However, implementation of an optimal Luenberger state
observer revealed a fatal flaw in the sensor suite: relying
on dead reckoning for position coordinates inevitably leads
to terminal drift. Future iterations must incorporate physical
position sensors to guarantee true autonomous operational
safety.
